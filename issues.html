Top Priorities

1) Affordability in Housing

As the next Representative for the 35th District, I will bring impactful rent control measures to our communities. Rent control is about putting power back where it belongs—We the People. We will reward developers when they build affordable housing and increase competition to stimulate growth and movement within our communities. It is important for a young man or woman to be able to find and afford their first apartment right out of high school.

2) Corporate Overhaul & Oversight

I demand accountability so corporate influence does not drown out everyday Americans. Oversight should protect consumers, workers, and taxpayers—full stop. For example, TransUnion has been sued for billions and is widely known for its failure to follow even the most basic consumer protection laws. Yet, to this day, it reports the “credibility” of millions of Americans while having none itself. This prevents millions of middle-class Americans from accessing credit, mortgages, and financial opportunity. The corporate hostile takeover ends now. We demand that the middle class be heard—today. Not tomorrow. Now.

3)Taking care of our veterans & Service Members

Active duty, Guard/Reserve, and retirees deserve consistent support on all fronts. I aim to modernize the support system in this country. Day one in Washington, I will introduce a bill that increase funding for civilian trianing in areas such as electricity carpentry, automotive technology, and more for are Nations troops. Making building and manufacturing in our Country popular again. No more heavy reliance on migrant work when we have hard working Americans looking to fill skilled labor positions.
