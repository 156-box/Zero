About WH R. Lane

WH R. Lane is a husband, father, and active-duty Army officer with over a decade of military service. Enlisted as an infantryman, rose to the rank of Staff Sergeant, and latered earned his commision.

Over the course of his career, WH has led company-sized and battalion-level elements within some of the Army's most operationally demanding organizations, including the 1st Special Forces group, 2nd Battalion 75th Ranger Regiment, and the 1st Armored Division.

In 2015, he deployed to Afghanistan with the 1st Armored Division. Serving as an infantryman embeded along side Special Forces elements. His leadership required managing complex situations Coordinating across agencies, and executing under pressure where accountability and percision was non-negotiable.

Lane holds a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science with an emphasis in Cybersecurity. He believes modern federal leadership must combine operational discipline with technological fluency to address infrastructure, national security, and economic stability.

WH is running for Congress to bring disciplined leadership, strategic execution and long-term accountability back to Washington - in service of working families and the future of California's 35th District.
